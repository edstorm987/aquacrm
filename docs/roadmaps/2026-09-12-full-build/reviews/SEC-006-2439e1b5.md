## P1 findings

1. Ownership is unsound for equivalent telephone formats and valid typed owners
   nested outside root `scope`/`owner`. A `+44` subject and equivalent `07`
   third-party Person were treated as exclusive, and a nested
   `payload.owner={kind:"person",id:"other"}` did not make the row ambiguous.
   `subjectAccessExport.ts` must reuse the canonical phone helper and perform a
   safe, getter-free, fully metered typed-owner traversal.
2. Allowlisted task `sourceId`/`sourceHref`, ledger `href` and finance
   `externalRef` strings are emitted verbatim. A direct probe exported an
   outsider email/address plus bank account and sort-code identifiers. Projection
   must redact unsafe values and count the omission truthfully.
3. Known SubjectRequest preparation, reviewer, evidence, delivery and fulfilment
   fields are silently dropped while `omittedFields=0` and
   `automatic-safe-subset-complete` are reported. Every known field must be
   safely projected or counted as withheld.
4. Delivery completion is not exact-retry idempotent. The first successful
   delivery fulfils the request and removes staged bytes; the same digest,
   method and evidence replay then returns `request_not_ready`. Durable replay
   identity must distinguish a confirmed prior success from a mismatched retry.
5. Work bounds are not tenant-local or comprehensive. Fifty thousand foreign
   task rows exhaust the record limit before one subject row. Nested unique
   owner claims follow a super-linear `includes` path and are missing from the
   advertised counter; the 16k probe took about 760 ms while reporting only five
   values visited. Tenant rejection must precede accounting and every traversal
   step must be metered/capped with Set/map linear accumulation.
