AAquaCRM

Privacy and cookies

Your details stay attached to the reason you shared them.

Enquiries

When you submit an enquiry, the contact details and message you provide are stored securely in AquaCRM so we can respond and manage the resulting relationship. This processing is separate from optional analytics or marketing cookies.

Cookie choices

Necessary storage records your privacy choice and supports essential site operation. You may separately allow preferences, analytics and marketing. Your choice is saved in this browser under aqua-cookie-preferences and can be changed with the Cookie settings button.

Aqua Tag

Aqua Tag remains inactive until a choice has been saved. It never captures the values typed into forms. Analytics events are sent only when analytics is allowed, and campaign or conversion events are sent only when marketing is allowed.

Contact

Questions about your information can be sent through the enquiry form on the AquaCRM contact page.
