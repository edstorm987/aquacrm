Common questions

Custom does not need to mean complicated.

We begin with the smallest useful version, make the decisions visible and give the system room to grow.

Is AquaCRM a product or a software company?

AquaCRM is the software company. Aqua CRM is one of our products: a modular operating platform for agencies and service businesses. Bespoke projects may use Aqua CRM, extend it, connect to it or become entirely independent products.

Do you only build CRM systems?

No. We build portals, EPOS, commerce, LMS, workforce, safety, reporting, automation and other operational software. The business problem determines the form.

Will you replace every system we already use?

Only when that earns its place. We can integrate useful existing tools, replace weak ones and create a clear control layer across the operation.

Can you travel to observe our operation?

Yes. On-site discovery is central to larger projects. Travel and the required access are agreed in the scope before the visit.

Can the software use our own brand?

Yes. Customer and team experiences can be designed around your identity, language, roles and workflow. AquaCRM remains the delivery and support relationship behind the system.

Can we begin with one process?

Yes. A focused workflow is often the best first release. We prove its value, learn from real use and then decide what should connect next.
