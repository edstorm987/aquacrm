# `scripts/audit-judgement-evidence.ts`

← [File index](../../files-index.md) · Area: Scripts — scripts/

**What it is:** The judgement path, exercised against real Radar data. Radar alerts are the ones nothing can resolve: no button returns a metric to its median. Their whole value is the evidence — the figure that moved, its history, and a plain-English reading of whether the movement means anything. This checks that is what actually comes back, rather than a statistical sentence with no numbers behind it.
